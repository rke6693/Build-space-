THE WHOLE TRICK

Pressure becomes lift.

  1. ↔
    Move

    Use A / D, the arrow keys, a gamepad stick, or the two large touch buttons.

  2. Squish

    Hold Space, a gamepad face button, or the yellow button while grounded. Gloob stores the pressure. Hold it through a landing and the charge carries over.

  3. ↑
    Spring

    Release to launch. Land on Needlers and Drifters, break amber locks, ride the moving shelves, and strike the Press while its eye is open.

There is no attack button. Gloob’s body is the verb.
